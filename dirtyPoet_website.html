脏诗是什么 What is dirtyPoet
"把手弄脏的人，
在写诗。" "Those who get their
hands dirty are writing poems."
脏，不是因为邋遢。是因为真实接触过什么之后留下的痕迹。每一个用自己方式认真生活的人，天生已经是一首不断的诗——平淡的、重复的、冒险的、激昂的……谁又能说哪种生活不是一首珍贵的作品？ Dirty is not careless. It's the mark left behind after genuine contact with something real. Everyone who lives seriously in their own way is, by nature, an ongoing poem — mundane, repetitive, adventurous, fierce. Who can say which life isn't a precious work?
Get your hands dirty. Write your way out. Get your hands dirty. Write your way out.
品牌世界 Brand World

我们相信的东西 What We Believe

01
脏
不怕弄脏，不是邋遢，是忠于真实接触过什么之后留下的痕迹。衣物和肌肤可以脏，但它反映的恰恰是一种内心的干净。 Not afraid to get dirty. It's not sloppiness — it's loyalty to the marks left after genuine contact. Clothes and skin can be dirty; what it reflects is a kind of inner cleanliness.
02
诗
不是坐下来写的那种。是用手写的——在岩壁上，在泥土里，在粉尘扬起来的那一刻。你不需要懂诗，你已经在写了。 Not the kind you sit down to write. Written with your hands — on rock walls, in soil, in the moment chalk dust rises. You don't need to understand poetry. You're already writing it.
03
见证
见证岩石产生裂痕，见证阳光穿过树叶，见证自己每一次失败和重来。脏诗不剪掉失误，因为那才是真正重要的部分。 Witness a rock crack. Witness sunlight through leaves. Witness every failure and restart. We don't cut the mistakes — those are the important parts.
04
野
不是野蛮，是野性。要有足够多粗粝的时刻，才能更好地感知生命的厚度和广度。不怕不精致，不怕不主流，不怕把手弄脏。 Not savage — wild. You need enough rough moments to feel the full weight of life. Not afraid of being imprecise, not mainstream, not clean. That's the point.
